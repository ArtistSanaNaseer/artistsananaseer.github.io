About the Artist

Traditional + Contemporary

Artist Statement

I am a visual artist working in a contemporary style, moving between realism and symbolism. My practice spans oil painting, miniature painting, sketching, calligraphy, murals, and photography, and I choose the medium according to the mood of the idea behind each piece.

I am drawn to nature and quiet moments: the way light falls, the texture of a surface, a fleeting expression. I build my work on balanced compositions and soft, restrained palettes, because I want my paintings to feel calm rather than loud. My work asks the viewer to pause, look closely, and feel something quietly.

My thesis series, 'Decline Beneath the Surface,' uses the crow as a symbol of the Mughal Empire's slow fade from glory, and that love of layered meaning runs through everything I make. Alongside my own work, I take on commissions and large-scale murals. In all of it, I aim for art that feels natural and honest, leaving room for each viewer to find their own meaning.
