About the Artist

Bachelor of Fine Arts — University of Sargodha

Artist Statement

I am a visual artist inspired by nature, quiet moments, and deep human emotions. My core practice revolves around Contemporary Miniature Painting (Gouache on Wasli), realistic oil landscapes, charcoal portraiture, calligraphy, and large-scale murals.

Through intricate detail and rich symbolism, my work connects historical techniques with modern concepts, inviting viewers to pause and observe quietly.
