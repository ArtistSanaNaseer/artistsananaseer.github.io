About the Artist

Bachelor of Fine Arts | University of Sargodha

Artist Statement


I am a visual artist inspired by nature, quiet moments, and the emotions that can be expressed through simple visual forms. My work mainly focuses on realism, where I use oil paints, graphite, charcoal, and gouache to create artworks that feel calm, soft, and emotionally connected. I enjoy observing natural details, light, textures, and expressions, and try to bring that same feeling into my paintings and drawings.

My artistic practice moves between realistic, symbolic, and sometimes abstract styles, depending on the idea or mood behind the work. I am especially drawn to balanced compositions and subtle color palettes that create a peaceful atmosphere for the viewer. Alongside paintings and commissioned portraits, I specialize in contemporary miniature art, calligraphy, wall murals, and sculpture.

Final Thesis Project

Gouache on Wasli | Miniature Painting

Decline Beneath The Surface

My thesis explores the concept of the Mughal Empire's decline, using the Crow as a central symbol. Through this work, I represent how power and glory gradually fade away, hidden beneath layers of history. This project combines my skills in symbolism and storytelling to show the silent transition from majesty to decay.

Decay Resting on Power
Gouache on Wasli | 7.5" x 7.5"
Shows acceptance of decline. The Empire had power to act but chose comfort and illusion over responsibility.
The Face of Authority
Gouache on Wasli | 8" x 5"
Reflects how physical strength cannot save an empire if its leadership ignores early signs of trouble.
Unnoticed Decline
Gouache on Wasli | 7" x 4.3"
Reflects how comfort causes powerful systems to ignore surrounding problems, allowing decline to become part of everyday life.
